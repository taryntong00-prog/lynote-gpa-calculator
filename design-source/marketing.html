Who needs this GPA calculator?

For students checking their grades and the people helping them understand the results.

College students

Semester results

Calculate your semester GPA from course grades and credits. Add a previous GPA when you want a cumulative result.

High school students

Weighted & unweighted GPA

Choose Regular, Honors, AP or IB for each course and compare the weighted and unweighted results.

Transfer students

Course and credit checks

Explore a GPA using the courses and credits you include. Follow your school’s rules when deciding which credits count.

Scholarship applicants

GPA requirement checks

Calculate your GPA before comparing it with a scholarship’s published requirement. Check which scale and courses it asks for.

Parents & guardians

Student progress

Review grades and credits together, and understand how each course contributes to a student’s overall GPA.

Teachers & advisors

Explain the calculation

Use the course breakdown and worked examples to explain grade points, credits and GPA to students.
