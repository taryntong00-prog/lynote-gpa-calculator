What is a GPA calculator?

A GPA calculator turns your course grades and credits into a grade point average. It helps you check one semester, combine earlier results, or compare weighted and unweighted GPA.

What does GPA mean?

GPA stands for grade point average. Each letter grade has a point value. A course with more credits has more influence on your average.

Lynote’s calculator works with college and high school letter grades. Start with your report card or transcript.

GPA scale: convert letter grades to points

Choose the grading scale your school uses. Compare Lynote’s three presets.

4.0 plus/minus
Keeps + and − grades. An A− is 3.7 and a B+ is 3.3.
4.33 scale
Uses the same points, except an A+ counts as 4.33 instead of 4.0.
Letters only
Ignores + and − signs. A+, A and A− all count as 4.0.

P, NP, W and I are excluded from GPA and the credits counted.

Semester vs. cumulative GPA

A semester GPA measures one term. A cumulative GPA combines your current courses with earlier study. The difference is which credits you include.

Before you compare your GPA

  1. 1

    Check the scale

    A 4.0-scale result and a weighted result are different measures.

  2. 2

    Check the courses included

    A semester, all school years and selected subject courses can produce different averages.

  3. 3

    Check the actual requirement

    Use the published rules for the program you have in mind. GPA alone does not determine admission or an award.

Why your GPA differs from your transcript

Even with the same courses, a different grading rule can change your result. Start by checking your school’s grade chart, then review these details.

Grade points and course bonuses
Lynote offers A+ values of 4.0 or 4.33. Honors adds 0.5; AP and IB add 1.0. Compare these values with your school’s rules.
Pass/fail and repeated courses
P, NP, W and I are excluded here. For retakes, your school may count both attempts or replace the earlier grade.
Credits included
Count each course once. Do not include the same course in both Previous GPA and your current course list.
Rounding
Lynote rounds to two decimal places. A school that truncates or uses different precision may show another result.

Your school’s transcript is the official record.

GPA Calculator FAQ

Answers to common questions about GPA calculations, grading scales and your results.

What is my GPA with all A’s and one B?

It depends on the number of courses and their credits. With four equally weighted courses, three A’s and one B give (4 + 4 + 4 + 3) ÷ 4 = 3.75 on the default 4.0 scale.

Can I calculate GPA without credit hours?

If every course counts equally, enter the same credit value for each. If the credits differ, use the actual course credits.

Does an F count toward GPA?

Yes. In this calculator, an F earns zero grade points, but its course credits are included. P, NP, W and I are excluded from both the points and the GPA credits.

Can I enter percentage grades?

The current calculator accepts letter grades. Use your school’s grading rules to convert a percentage to the correct letter grade first.

Where can I find my official GPA?

Check your transcript or your school’s student portal. This calculator estimates GPA from the grades and credits you enter.

Can I download my results?

Yes. Download a CSV or choose Print / PDF to print or save through your browser. Your entries are not automatically saved after a page refresh.

Can I include a previous weighted or unweighted GPA?

Yes. In high school mode, select the type of your previous GPA. It is combined only with the matching result type. The other result covers your entered classes.

Is the GPA calculator free?

You can start with 50 free calculations without signing up. Signed-in users receive a one-time allowance of 100 free calculations. Additional calculations follow Lynote’s credit rules.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Use the GPA calculator in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone.
